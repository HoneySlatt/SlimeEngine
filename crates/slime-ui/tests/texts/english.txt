The editor shows a hierarchy of entities on the left, an inspector on the right, and the view of the world in the middle.
Every panel can be docked, undocked, resized, or grouped into tabs, and the layout is saved with the project.
Text in the interface is shaped with the same engine that games use for their menus, so that a translation tested in the editor looks the same in the game.
Long names are wrapped at word boundaries, and a name that does not fit on one line is broken at the last opportunity before the edge.
Numbers such as 3.14159, 1,024 and 65,536 keep their precision when they are edited.
Screen readers receive a tree of nodes with roles, names and bounds, updated as the interface changes.
A quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog while twelve wizards box five jumping quails.
A label too long for its box ends with an ellipsis… where it is cut.
