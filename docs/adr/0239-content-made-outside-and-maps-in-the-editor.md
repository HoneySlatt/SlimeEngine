# 0239. What a game's content needs: importers, characters, large sources, and maps in the editor

- Status: Accepted; amends [0209](0209-platforms-the-editor-and-agents.md) (its importers and
  Blender's live link placed in stages; Alembic, OpenEXR, BVH and reliefs added; large sources
  kept in versions from Stage 9b) and [0217](0217-worlds-by-density-generated-and-destroyed.md)
  (maps made in the editor, reliefs and placements imported)
- Date: 2026-11-11

## Context

The owner asked on 2026-11-11 what making games of the large studios' class would need once the
roadmap is done: assets, characters, animation, cinematics, open worlds. The roadmap builds the
engine's systems for each. A reading of the roadmap, the ADRs and the cooker found what it leaves
out:

- The cooker imports glTF, PNG and JPEG alone (`crates/slime-cook/README.md`).
  [ADR-0209](0209-platforms-the-editor-and-agents.md) made FBX and OpenUSD optional importers, and
  linked Blender live to the editor; no stage of the roadmap holds them.
- Motion captured comes as FBX or BVH; simulations computed beforehand, cloth or destruction, as
  Alembic caches; images of high range as OpenEXR; reliefs as height maps, images whose pixels are
  heights, or as elevation data.
- Retargeting, motion matching and faces driven by voice (Stage 9b) each need to know a
  character's bones and expressions. No stage says what characters share, nor how a person scanned
  or sculpted becomes a character with a skeleton and a face that moves.
- The faces' capture with a plain camera is planned (Stage 9b); a body's capture is not.
- A game of that class keeps sources of several terabytes. ADR-0209 gives the editor Git with LFS;
  a shared cache of cooked data and cooking spread over machines are in the horizon, after 1.0,
  when a small team making such content needs them from its first slice.
- The roadmap builds dialogue, quests and the sequencer; the tools of those who write and design,
  a writer's tree of lines, a level blocked out in simple shapes before its art, a camera held by
  hand for cinematics, are named nowhere.
- How a map is made is left open: [ADR-0217](0217-worlds-by-density-generated-and-destroyed.md)
  plans graphs of rules for terrain, rivers, roads, vegetation and villages, their result frozen,
  then changed by hand, without saying whether the editor or another tool holds the map. Blender
  holds neither cells, levels of detail nor streaming, and cannot show a world of many km² as the
  game draws it.

The tools' versions, licences and readers in Rust were not checked here: each is checked at the
stage that builds its importer.

## Decision

The owner's decisions of 2026-11-11.

**Importers come with the stage whose system reads them.** glTF stays the native format
(ADR-0209). Animation clips from FBX and BVH, with animation's core at Stage 7. Images of high
range from OpenEXR at Stage 9a. Scenes from OpenUSD and caches from Alembic at Stage 9b. Reliefs
from 16-bit PNG, OpenEXR and elevation data, and placements from OpenUSD, at Stage 10a. Each
reader, a crate, a library bound or one of the engine's own, is chosen at its stage.

**A skeleton and a face's expressions shared by a game's characters,** decided at Stage 9b's
start: what every character has or maps onto, so that retargeting, motion matching, faces driven
by voice and the track of combat work on each without being made again; and the way from a person
scanned or sculpted to a character with its skeleton and its face. It is a structure, never a
library of faces: faces come from people captured or from artists (ADR-0215).

**Blender's live link, at Stage 9b,** in the chain of production. Blender makes the pieces:
models, sculpts, rigs and animation made by hand. Other tools, Houdini or a terrain generator,
reach the engine through the files above.

**A body's capture, at Stage 9b,** with the faces': clips from a video, from a suit of sensors or
from an optical studio's files, imported and cleaned in the editor.

**Large sources from Stage 9b.** Sources of terabytes kept in versions, Git with LFS, Lore and
Perforce measured on a game's sources, and a shared cache of cooked data, so that nobody cooks
again what another has cooked; cooking spread over several machines at Stage 10a, with its budget
of cooking time per km². Locks on assets and distributed builds stay in the horizon.

**The tools of those who write and design, at Stage 9b:** a writer's tool for trees of lines and
the world's bible, the characters, places and facts that lines and quests name; a level blocked
out in simple shapes before its art; a camera held by hand for the sequencer.

**Maps are made in the editor,** never in Blender. A relief generated by a graph, or imported; its
rivers, roads and cliffs drawn as curves; its zones painted, each with what grows or stands there;
graphs that fill the zones with the pieces made in Blender, their result frozen as placements; then
the places made by hand, and every placement changed by hand. Cooking cuts the map into cells and
computes what is seen far away. Agents drive each step through the engine's MCP server.

## Alternatives considered

- **Houdini as the maps' main generator, the editor assembling only:** a mature tool, the
  industry's, ready now; but paid and closed, its results reached by round trips instead of seen
  live, agents driving it worse, and the editor's lead on the creation bench lost.
- **Maps in the editor, without importers:** the simplest to build; every team made to remake its
  reliefs, which a public engine cannot ask.
- **Maps in Blender:** no cells, no streaming, no image as the game draws it.
- **FBX alone through Blender, as a conversion:** no importer to hold, but a step by hand on every
  clip captured; ADR-0209's optional importer stays.
- **No skeleton shared, each character its own:** free for artists, but retargeting, motion
  matching and faces made again for each character.
- **Large sources and the shared cache left to the horizon:** nothing to build before 1.0, and a
  small team's first slice of content blocked by its own size.
- **The tools of writing and design left to Stage 11:** the engine's systems first, but content
  made without them until then.

## Consequences

- [`ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) gains the importers in Stages 7, 9a, 9b and 10a; the shared
  skeleton and face, Blender's live link, a body's capture, large sources and the tools of writing
  and design in Stage 9b; maps made in the editor and cooking spread over machines in Stage 10a;
  two open decisions, the shared skeleton and face, and the versions of large sources; the horizon
  keeps locks and distributed builds alone.
- Content beyond the engine stays the games': people scanned and actors under contract and
  consent, capture sessions, libraries of assets and sounds whose licences are checked for each
  asset.
