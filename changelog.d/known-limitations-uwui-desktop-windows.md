- On Windows, uwui's desktop host takes no keys, focus nor touch before M6d.7c2, and tells no
  image's time, no covered window and no resume while its window rests before M6d.7c3 (ADR-0317).
- On Windows, a restart or a log-off ends an application on uwui's desktop host without asking
  it to close: its unsaved work is lost, until M6d.7c3 tells the application of it.
