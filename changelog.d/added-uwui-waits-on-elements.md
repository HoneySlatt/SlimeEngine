- uwui keeps waits on a target's elements across frames, with the feature `agents`
  (`uwui::agents::Waits`): a role, a text that the name holds, states that hold or not, present
  or absent, met at once or checked after each of the target's frames on the elements that changed
  alone; an element removed or a dialog closed ends a wait for its absence. A check counts the
  elements that it read (ADR-0312).
